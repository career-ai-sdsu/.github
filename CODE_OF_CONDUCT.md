# Code of Conduct

Career AI is a collaborative student project. Contributors are expected to work respectfully, professionally, and constructively.

## Expectations

- Treat teammates with respect.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Make space for questions and different levels of experience.
- Communicate blockers and disagreements constructively.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's personal information without permission.
- Do not use project data, resumes, or other materials outside their intended purpose.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, or sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Resolving Problems

When possible, project disagreements should first be handled directly and respectfully within the team.

Issues involving harassment, safety, or serious misconduct should be raised with the appropriate project lead or SDSU AI Club leadership.
